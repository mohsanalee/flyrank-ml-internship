## 2. Where I Chose Real Captures Over AI

For SecureScan AI, I used real screenshots of the working application rather than AI-generated representations. The code-input and security-results screenshots are especially important because they provide direct evidence of the actual system and its output.

I also used a real photograph of myself instead of an AI-generated portrait. Generated visuals were limited to connective material such as the workflow/architecture explanation.

---

## 3. Rejected Generated Image

I rejected a generic futuristic cybersecurity illustration with glowing servers, neon interfaces, and abstract AI elements.

It looked visually impressive but did not communicate anything specific about SecureScan AI. I chose real project captures instead because they provide stronger and more credible evidence of the actual work.

---

## Curation Principle

Real project evidence is prioritized wherever the portfolio needs to prove actual functionality. Generated imagery is used only when it helps explain or connect the content, while personal representation uses a real photograph.
